What to Expect

Iceland in July — tips for the family

  • Temperatures: 10–18°C — cool but very pleasant for touring
  • Midnight sun — it never fully gets dark in July!
  • Rain is possible any day — always pack waterproof layers
  • Wind can be strong at exposed coastal viewpoints
  • Pack sunscreen — UV is surprisingly strong even through cloud
  • Waterproof jacket & trousers — absolutely essential
  • Warm base layers — fleece or merino wool recommended
  • Comfortable walking shoes with grip (no heels!)
  • Swimsuit & quick-dry towel for every geothermal stop
  • Sunglasses — glare from glaciers and water is intense
  • Small daypack for snacks, water, extra layers
  • Fill up petrol at every opportunity — stations are sparse
  • Speed limits: 90 km/h highway, 80 km/h gravel roads
  • Watch for sheep on roads — they have right of way!
  • Download offline Google Maps before leaving each city
  • Book geothermal baths in advance — they sell out in July
  • Check road.is for conditions before each day's drive
  • No F-roads (rough 4WD tracks) anywhere in this itinerary
  • All hotels selected for comfort and accessibility
  • Geothermal baths are therapeutic — great for joints
  • Daily driving kept under 4 hours maximum
  • Pace is flexible — optional stops can be skipped easily
  • Rest days built in at Hotel Glymur (2 nights)
  • Grocery shops (Krónan, Bónus) are affordable — stock up daily
  • Lamb, fish & skyr are local must-tries
  • Iceland is near-cashless — card accepted everywhere
  • N1 petrol stations have great fast food (try Pylsur hot dogs!)
  • Most restaurants can accommodate dietary requirements

Safe travels, family. 🧡
